> **ℹ️ Fork Notes** — This is a fork of [DeanChensj/jiangshan-map](https://github.com/DeanChensj/jiangshan-map) (GPL-3.0). All credit for the atlas engine, historical data and design belongs to the upstream author. This fork applies two editorial changes: **(1) de-modernization — the atlas now ends at 1912 (the end of imperial China)**. Removed: Republic of China / People's Republic of China periods and phases, the modern province-boundary & province-name layers, the modern X-Ray lens, and modern-era landmark events & regime profiles. Deep links (`?year=`) clamp to 1912. Everything else — engine, historical data, place genealogy, journeys, quiz, i18n — is upstream as-is. **(2) War Sandbox (战争沙盘)** — ported from the 舆图千年 (yutu-qiannian) project, rebuilt as a fork-native SVG module: campaign replay driven by the timeline, march arrows width-encoded by recorded army strength (with per-figure source citations), battle diamonds colored by the winning side, schematic front-zone shifts, and a video-player-style transport (play/pause, step, restart, speed, step position). Nineteen campaigns across 2,300 years: the Jingnan Campaign (1399-1402, 28 steps), Changping (262-259 BCE), Julu (207 BCE), Gaixia (202 BCE), Kunyang (23 CE), Guandu (200), Red Cliffs (208), Yiling (222), the An-Shi Rebellion (755-763), Jingkang (1125-27), Yue Fei (1140), Mobei (119 BCE), Yamen (1279), Tumu and the defense of Beijing (1449), Sarhu (1619), the Taiping war (1851-64), and the First Sino-Japanese War (1894-95).
